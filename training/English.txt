### Examination-in-Chief of PW-3 (Complainant) by Learned APP

**Public Prosecutor:** Shri Vikram Singh, do you know the deceased, Deepak Sharma?

**Witness (PW-3):** Yes, sir. Deepak Sharma was my maternal cousin and also my business partner in our wholesale hardware shop at Sarafa Bazaar.

**Public Prosecutor:** Do you know the accused persons standing in the dock?

**Witness (PW-3):** Yes, I can identify them. The person standing on the left in the blue shirt is Ramesh Kumar. The person standing next to him is Suresh.

**Judge:** Let the record reflect that the witness has correctly identified Accused Number 1, Ramesh Kumar, and Accused Number 2, Suresh.

**Public Prosecutor:** Vikram ji, where were you on the night of 14th November 2020 at about 9:30 PM?

**Witness (PW-3):** On 14th November 2020, around 9:15 PM, Deepak and I closed our shop in Sarafa Bazaar. We were carrying the cash collection of the week, approximately two lakh fifty thousand rupees, in a black leather bag. We were returning home on our Bajaj Pulsar motorcycle. Deepak was riding, and I was on the pillion holding the bag.

**Public Prosecutor:** What happened when you reached near the railway underpass near Dharampeth?

**Witness (PW-3):** At around 9:40 PM, as we slowed down due to the waterlogging near the underpass, a black Hero Splendor motorcycle overtook us from the left and abruptly intercepted our path. Two persons were on the motorcycle. Both had wrapped mufflers around their necks, but their faces were uncovered.

**Public Prosecutor:** What did they do immediately after intercepting your motorcycle?

**Witness (PW-3):** Accused Number 1, Ramesh, got down holding a country-made firearm—a *katta*. He pointed it directly at Deepak’s chest and shouted, *"Saara maal aur bag chupchap de do, nahi toh goli maar denge."* Accused Number 2, Suresh, came towards me brandishing a large rampuri knife. Deepak resisted and pushed Ramesh away to defend us. At that exact moment, Ramesh fired a point-blank shot into Deepak’s left upper chest.

**Defense Counsel:** Objection, Your Honour! The witness is giving a tutored and dramatic narrative. The prosecution cannot lead the witness into making categorical assertions regarding trajectory or firearm specifications unless corroborated.

**Judge:** Objection overruled. The witness is narrating what he personally observed through his senses as per Section 60 of the Indian Evidence Act. Let the deposition continue. Note the objection.

**Public Prosecutor:** What happened after the gunshot was fired?

**Witness (PW-3):** Deepak collapsed on the asphalt road, bleeding profusely from his chest. I screamed for help. Accused Number 2, Suresh, snatched the black leather bag containing two lakh fifty thousand rupees from my lap. Ramesh threatened to shoot me if I moved. Both of them immediately mounted their motorcycle and fled towards the Ring Road.

**Public Prosecutor:** Was there sufficient light at the place of occurrence for you to identify the assailants?

**Witness (PW-3):** Yes. The streetlights on the municipal pole right above the underpass were functional, and the high-beam headlight of our own motorcycle remained on throughout the incident. I saw their faces clearly from a distance of less than three feet.

**Public Prosecutor:** What did you do next?

**Witness (PW-3):** I immediately dialed 100 from my mobile phone. Within fifteen minutes, the PCR van arrived. We shifted Deepak to the Government Medical College and Hospital, where the attending casualty medical officer declared him brought dead. Thereafter, the police recorded my statement at the hospital police post.

**Public Prosecutor:** Look at this document. Is this your signature at point A?

**Witness (PW-3):** Yes, this is my signature. This is the statement I gave, which was treated as the FIR.

**Public Prosecutor:** Your Honour, I pray that the written complaint be marked as Exhibit P-1.

**Judge:** Let the First Information Report dated 15th November 2020, registered under Crime No. 582/2020 at Police Station Sitabuldi, be marked as Exhibit P-1.

**Public Prosecutor:** Vikram ji, did you later participate in any Test Identification Parade?

**Witness (PW-3):** Yes. On 28th November 2020, I was called to Central Jail by the Executive Magistrate. There were eight non-suspects along with the accused. I identified Ramesh Kumar without any hesitation.

**Public Prosecutor:** Your Honour, the TIP memo is already marked as Exhibit P-14 through the Executive Magistrate. That concludes the Examination-in-Chief. The witness is tendered for cross-examination.

### Cross-Examination of PW-3 by Learned Defense Counsel

**Defense Counsel:** Shri Vikram Singh, how long have you been wearing prescription spectacles?

**Witness (PW-3):** Since the past ten years.

**Defense Counsel:** What is your power?

**Witness (PW-3):** Minus 2.5 in both eyes for distant vision.

**Defense Counsel:** On the night of 14th November 2020, when your motorcycle allegedly slipped or stopped near the railway underpass, were your spectacles intact on your face?

**Witness (PW-3):** Yes, I was wearing them.

**Defense Counsel:** I put it to you that November nights under the Dharampeth railway bridge are notoriously dark, and there was heavy smog and fog on that particular night.

**Witness (PW-3):** There was mild chill, but no dense fog. Visibility was clear.

**Defense Counsel:** Is it not a fact that in the Spot Panchnama, Exhibit P-4, there is no mention whatsoever of any functional municipal streetlight within a radius of fifty meters?

**Witness (PW-3):** I do not know what the police wrote in the Panchnama, but the light was burning.

**Defense Counsel:** You stated before the Court today that Accused Number 1 fired from a point-blank range of less than three feet. Did you state this fact to the police in your statement recorded under Section 161 of the Code of Criminal Procedure?

**Witness (PW-3):** Yes, I told the police officer everything.

**Defense Counsel:** Your Honour, I crave leave to confront the witness with his previous statement recorded under Section 161 CrPC, marked as Document D-2 for identification.

**Judge:** Leave granted. Hand over the certified copy of the Section 161 statement to the witness.

**Defense Counsel:** Read portion marked ‘A to B’ of your Section 161 statement. Does it state "fired from point-blank range", or does it merely state: "I heard a loud explosion like a tyre burst while I was on the ground"?

**Witness (PW-3):** It says here... "I heard a loud sound." But I did tell the Investigating Officer that he shot him right in front of me.

**Defense Counsel:** So this is an improvement that you are making for the very first time before this Court to implicate my client, Ramesh Kumar?

**Witness (PW-3):** No, that is incorrect. It was chaotic that night.

**Defense Counsel:** I put it to you that you were in heavy financial debt to private financiers in Itwari Market, and Deepak Sharma had initiated proceedings under Section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act against you in respect of three dishonoured cheques. Isn't that true?

**Public Prosecutor:** Objection, Your Honour! Relevance? This is character assassination without foundational pleadings.

**Defense Counsel:** Your Honour, this directly goes to the motive and credibility of the witness under Section 146 of the Evidence Act. We are setting up a specific defence that this witness had deep-rooted financial enmity with the deceased, and the alleged robbery is a fabricated story to misappropriate the two lakh fifty thousand rupees and eliminate his creditor.

**Judge:** Objection noted. The question is allowed to test the veracity and motive of the witness. Answer the question, Mr. Vikram Singh.

**Witness (PW-3):** Deepak and I had some business disputes regarding partnership accounts, but they were being resolved mutually. No formal case under 138 was pending on that date; only legal notices had been exchanged.

**Defense Counsel:** So you admit receiving statutory demand notices from the deceased?

**Witness (PW-3):** Yes, our advocates were handling it.

**Defense Counsel:** Is it not true that the photographs of Ramesh Kumar and Suresh were widely published in the local daily newspaper *Dainik Samachar* on 22nd November 2020, six days prior to the alleged Test Identification Parade in Central Jail?

**Witness (PW-3):** I do not read *Dainik Samachar*. I read *The Hitavada*.

**Defense Counsel:** I put it to you that the Investigating Officer showed both accused persons to you in the Sitabuldi Police Station lockup on the morning of 24th November, and your identification in the jail TIP was completely staged and bogus.

**Witness (PW-3):** No, this is completely false. I never visited the lockup.

**Defense Counsel:** I put it to you that you did not see who attacked Deepak Sharma, you were not present at the spot, you fabricated this false story in collusion with the local police, and you are deposing falsely against Ramesh Kumar and Suresh to usurp the partnership funds.

**Witness (PW-3):** It is totally false. I saw them shoot my brother.

**Defense Counsel:** Nothing further for this witness, Your Honour.

**Judge:** Any re-examination, Learned APP?

**Public Prosecutor:** None, Your Honour. PW-3 may be discharged.

**Judge:** PW-3 is discharged. Who is the next witness?

### Examination-in-Chief of PW-4 (Investigating Officer) by Learned APP

**Public Prosecutor:** The prosecution calls PW-4, Sub-Inspector Mahendra Patil.

**Court Master:** Step into the witness box. Take the oath.

**Witness (PW-4):** I swear by God that the evidence I shall give to the Court shall be the truth, the whole truth, and nothing but the truth.

**Judge:** Record name, age, designation, and present posting.

**Witness (PW-4):** Mahendra Patil, aged 46 years, Police Sub-Inspector, currently posted at Economic Offences Wing, Nagpur City. On 14th November 2020, I was posted as Assistant Sub-Inspector at Sitabuldi Police Station.

**Public Prosecutor:** Sub-Inspector Patil, please explain your role in Crime Number 582 of 2020.

**Witness (PW-4):** On 15th November 2020, upon registration of FIR under Section 302 and 394 IPC, the investigation was entrusted to Inspector Deshmukh, and I was directed to act as the assisting officer. I conducted the spot panchnama in the presence of two independent panchas, Shri Manohar Joshi and Shri Abdul Razzaq.

**Public Prosecutor:** What did you seize from the scene of the crime?

**Witness (PW-4):** We seized one empty cartridge case of 7.65 mm caliber from the roadside, marked as Article 1. We lifted blood-stained earth and sample ordinary earth from the spot. We also seized the blood-soaked helmet belonging to the deceased. All articles were sealed in separate paper parcels with the seal of ‘Police Station Sitabuldi’ and signed by the panchas.

**Public Prosecutor:** Look at this Panchnama. Is this your signature?

**Witness (PW-4):** Yes. This is the Spot Panchnama drawn between 06:00 AM and 07:30 AM on 15th November. It is marked as Exhibit P-4.

**Public Prosecutor:** Did you effect any recovery pursuant to the arrest of Accused Number 1, Ramesh Kumar?

**Witness (PW-4):** Yes. On 20th November 2020, while in police custody remand, Accused Ramesh Kumar voluntarily made a disclosure statement under Section 27 of the Indian Evidence Act. He stated that he had hidden the country-made pistol (*katta*) used in the commission of the crime, along with one live cartridge, inside the hollow of a banyan tree behind the abandoned octroi post near Amravati Road.

**Public Prosecutor:** Was this disclosure reduced into writing?

**Witness (PW-4):** Yes, in the presence of two panch witnesses, Shri Nitin Kale and Shri Santosh Gokhale. The memorandum is Exhibit P-18.

**Public Prosecutor:** What followed thereafter?

**Witness (PW-4):** The accused led the police party and the panchas in the official vehicle to the said spot. He walked up to the banyan tree, reached inside the hollow trunk, and produced a country-made firearm with wooden grips wrapped in an oily cloth. We drew the Seizure Panchnama on the spot, sealed the weapon, and affixed the seal. The recovery panchnama is Exhibit P-19, bearing my signature at point B.

**Public Prosecutor:** Did you forward the seized firearm and the empty cartridge recovered from the spot to the Forensic Science Laboratory?

**Witness (PW-4):** Yes, through Constable Billa No. 1402 on 23rd November 2020, along with the forwarding letter from the Judicial Magistrate First Class. The FSL Ballistics Report dated 12th January 2021 confirms that the empty cartridge recovered from the scene was fired from the very country-made pistol recovered at the instance of Accused Ramesh Kumar. The FSL report is Exhibit P-22.

**Public Prosecutor:** The witness is tendered for cross-examination.

### Cross-Examination of PW-4 by Learned Defense Counsel

**Defense Counsel:** Sub-Inspector Patil, tell this Court: how far is the Sitabuldi Police Station from the abandoned octroi post on Amravati Road?

**Witness (PW-4):** Approximately nine kilometers.

**Defense Counsel:** When you allegedly left the police station for the recovery of the weapon under Section 27, did you make an entry in the General Diary (Station Diary) regarding your departure, mentioning the vehicle number and the names of the panchas?

**Witness (PW-4):** The entry was made by the Duty Officer.

**Defense Counsel:** Have you produced that Station Diary entry or filed a certified extract thereof with the chargesheet under Section 173(2) CrPC?

**Witness (PW-4):** No, it was not annexed with the chargesheet.

**Defense Counsel:** So there is no documentary proof before this Court that you, the accused, and the panchas actually departed the police station at the recorded time?

**Witness (PW-4):** The panchnama itself mentions the time of departure and arrival.

**Defense Counsel:** The panchnama is your own document, Officer! I am asking about the official statutory register under Section 44 of the Police Act. Was it suppressed because no such departure ever took place?

**Public Prosecutor:** The officer has already explained that it was not annexed. He is badgering the witness.

**Judge:** Counsel may rephrase, but the omission of the Station Diary extract is a material matter of record. Proceed.

**Defense Counsel:** Inspector, who are these two panchas, Nitin Kale and Santosh Gokhale? Are they local residents of the area near the abandoned octroi post?

**Witness (PW-4):** No, they were summoned from near the police station premises.

**Defense Counsel:** Exactly! They are stock witnesses of the Sitabuldi Police Station, isn't it? Isn't it a fact that Nitin Kale is also a panch witness in Crime No. 210/2019 and Crime No. 441/2020 registered at your very police station?

**Witness (PW-4):** I cannot recall without checking the records of other cases.

**Defense Counsel:** You know very well he is. Did you request any shopkeepers or residents living near the octroi naka to join as independent witnesses before conducting the search and seizure under Section 100(4) of the CrPC?

**Witness (PW-4):** It was secluded. There were no active shops nearby at that hour.

**Defense Counsel:** Did you offer yourself or your accompanying police personnel for a personal search by the panchas or the accused before reaching the banyan tree?

**Witness (PW-4):** I don't remember if a formal search memo was recorded.

**Defense Counsel:** It was not recorded because you brought that unregistered firearm directly from the *Malkhana* of the police station and planted it in the case to frame Ramesh Kumar. What do you have to say?

**Witness (PW-4):** That is completely false and denied. The recovery was genuine.

**Defense Counsel:** Between 15th November 2020 and 23rd November 2020, where was the recovered cartridge kept? In whose custody?

**Witness (PW-4):** It was deposited in the Malkhana under the charge of the Head Constable Moharrir.

**Defense Counsel:** Did you file the Register Number 19 of the Malkhana to show that the seal remained intact and the parcel was not tampered with prior to being dispatched to the Ballistic Expert?

**Witness (PW-4):** The Malkhana in-charge maintains that. It is not part of this record.

**Defense Counsel:** I put it to you, Officer Patil, that the chain of custody of the ballistic evidence is completely broken, the Section 27 recovery is fictitious and manufactured within the four walls of the police station, and you have falsely implicated Accused Number 1 and 2 at the behest of local business rivals.

**Witness (PW-4):** I deny the suggestion in its entirety. The investigation was fair, impartial, and strictly according to law.

**Defense Counsel:** That is all for this witness.
